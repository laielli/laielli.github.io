Scaling from 3K examples / 10 tasks to 30K / 40 tasks raised zero-shot precision 5× (0.03 → 0.15) on held-out tasks spanning intersections, traffic lights, distraction, and drowsiness, while lights-and-signs precision rose 0.2 → 0.6.
