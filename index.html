Michael Laielli

Machine Learning Engineer · previously Staff Research Engineer, Netradyne

I build computer-vision and video systems that learn efficiently from people — human-in-the-loop, label-efficient ML, from architecture through on-device deployment.

At Netradyne (2021–2026) I led the deep-learning model R&D behind Drowsy Driver Alert, now running on several hundred thousand edge devices across 8+ countries, and later fine-tuned vision-language models on driving video. My through-line is getting the most signal from the least human-labeled data: earlier I proposed and co-created the xView satellite-imagery benchmark at the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency (540+ citations), and did a UC Berkeley PhD (BAIR) on label-efficient computer vision and human-AI interaction.

Co-inventor on the pending patent. computer-vision · video · edge · distillation
xView satellite-imagery benchmark — NGA 2018 Proposed and co-created a landmark large-scale overhead-imagery object-detection dataset and public benchmark (540+ citations), scoped through discovery interviews with dozens of imagery analysts. computer-vision · datasets · remote-sensing
LabelAR — UC Berkeley (UIST 2019) 2019 Led an augmented-reality interface (HoloLens + ARKit) for fast computer-vision data collection — ~2.6× faster than post-hoc labeling at comparable accuracy. Owned the AR build, detector training, and user study. computer-vision · hci · ar · label-efficient
VLM scaling laws for driving video — Netradyne 2025 Fine-tuned Qwen2.5-VL on dual cabin- and road-facing driving video, curating the training and eval sets. Zero-shot performance scales log-linearly with training data: 5× precision on held-out tasks (0.03 → 0.15) from 3K to 30K examples. Labeling tooling built with LLM coding agents. vlm · video · scaling-laws · data-curation
Text-to-video retrieval — Netradyne 2025 Fine-tuned CLIP-family models (LongCLIP, SigLIP, Perception Encoder) for the internal video search the ML team used to build training and eval datasets: +10–20 points top-1 retrieval on hard, motion-based concepts. vlm · retrieval · video · data-curation
